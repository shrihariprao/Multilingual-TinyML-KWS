Critical interpretation

The 87.5% sample-split result is not reliable evidence of generalization to unseen utterance families. The family-disjoint evaluation falls to 20% accuracy and 0.169 macro-F1. The experiment uses synthetic samples, contains only 40 held-out family-disjoint samples, and does not establish real human-speech performance.
