Research limitations

Do not present sample-level accuracy alone as evidence of generalization. The pilot is synthetic, uses small test sets, and the family-disjoint score is substantially lower. Native-speaker text verification is incomplete; physical TinyML device latency and energy have not been measured. The reported TFLite latency belongs to Colab interpreter invocation only.
